Plastic Balls are an entity and item in Escape the Backrooms. They appear to be ball pit balls.

Appearance

Plastic Balls appear to be plastic polymer balls found in ball pits that are apparently sentient. They come in the colors red, green, blue, and yellow. As an item, they only come in blue.

Location

Plastic balls can be found in:

  • Level 421 - Plastic Mariana

Use

Using the Item

Upon using, the player throws the ball, which can be re-picked up and thrown again.

Purpose

The purpose of the balls is to throw them into the basketball hoops in the basketball hoop room in the Plastic Mariana of Level 421 and the one basketball hoop in the Bruce's Discovery Center area. If a ball gets into a hoop in the basketball hoop room, the light above it will shine. Make all four lights shine, and the exit to Overgrowth will unlock.

Behavior

While plastic balls do not behave since they are just plastic balls that are only sentient, a white Light Snake exists in the Plastic Mariana of Level 421, which means the balls change their color to white to show the snake being there. It roams around the balls aimlessly and will lead to a circle once all four lighthouse lights are on that must be walked into by a player to get to the basketball hoop room to escape Level 421. A Light Snake might also be present in the fourth plastic ball chamber in the M.E.G. base in Level 421, as its different color changing balls changing to show a line shape.

Trivia

Lore & theories

  • A description of Level 421 that explains the level and the balls lore was given with the level name reveal, "The M.E.G were researching a new entity - a living, sentient, plastic polymer ball. Now it's escaped into a children's hospital, and a no-clip zone has been located inside Bruce's Discovery Centre. Where will it lead?".
    • This is the only thing that confirms the ball pit balls that aren't the Light Snake as entities.
    • In this description, Center is spelled as "Centre", due to Secret Mode being an England studio.
