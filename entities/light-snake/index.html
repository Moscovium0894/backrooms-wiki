Light Snake

Friendly Plastic Balls

The Light Snake is an entity that changes the colors of the plastic balls to white in the Plastic Mariana of Level 421 to show where it's at, although it can't be seen on the paths players have walked through due to it only being seen on the balls that can be pushed that cover the lower inactive ones.

Survival card

DO

  • Turn on the lighthouses.
  • Follow the Light Snake when all the lighthouses are on.
  • Go into the Light Snake's circle when it appears.

DON'T

  • Follow it without having turned on all four lighthouses if you're playing solo.
  • Stay near it if Bruce is near or chasing you.
Sighted in
Level 421

Appearance

The Light Snake is an entity that changes the colors of the plastic balls to white in the Plastic Mariana of Level 421 to show where it's at, although it can't be seen on the paths players have walked through due to it only being seen on the balls that can be pushed that cover the lower inactive ones. The shape the white balls form resembles a long line like a snake.

Location

This entity can be found in:

  • Level 421 - Plastic Mariana

Behavior

The Light Snake resides in the Plastic Mariana of Level 421. It will aimlessly move through the balls and it doesn't do anything at first. If all four lighthouse lights are on, the Light Snake will form a circle and the rest of the snake will lead to it. If a player goes into the circle, they will noclip into the basketball hoop room of Level 421.

Do's & Don'ts

Do's

  • Turn on the lighthouses.
  • Follow the Light Snake when all the lighthouses are on.
  • Go into the Light Snake's circle when it appears.

Don'ts

  • Follow it without having turned on all four lighthouses if you're playing solo.
  • Stay near it if Bruce is near or chasing you.

Trivia

  • Another Light Snake might be present in the fourth plastic ball chamber in the M.E.G. base in Level 421, as its different color changing balls changing to show a line shape and it functions the exact same just with a different color, although this is unconfirmed.
    • Something that proves this even more is from a video on the Escape the Backrooms TikTok account, in which the changing color of the balls is white for some reason.
