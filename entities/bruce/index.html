Level 421

Bruce

Appearance

Bruce simply appears to be a ginormous shark from the real world that swims in the Plastic Mariana of Level 421. It seems to be a monstrous form of Level 421's mascot who is also named Bruce.

Location

This entity can be found in:

  • Level 421 - Plastic Mariana

Behavior

Bruce swims through the Plastic Balls in the Plastic Mariana of Level 421 with its fin seen above the balls. Sometimes, its fin will go down into the balls, meaning a player can only determine where it's at through sound and the movement of the balls. Bruce cannot kill anyone when its fin is under the balls. If Bruce figures out where a player is, it will start swimming towards them. If a player runs into Bruce, it will kill them.

Do's & Don'ts

Do's

  • Look for the fin before returning to the ball pit if you're on an object or in a lighthouse.
  • Plan out each structure you go to and get there as fast as possible.
  • Get onto the closest structure if it starts chasing you.
  • Start running if you hear it.

Don'ts

  • Roam the ball pit not knowing where it is.
  • Walk through the ball pit recklessly.
  • Walk into the fin.

Trivia

The entity

  • Bruce has a lot of similarities to The Thing on Level 7.
    • They're both aquatic creatures that swim.
    • They both attack a player from underneath.
    • The idea that something of Bruce can be seen above the balls and that it can start swimming towards a player are unused ideas for the Thing on Level 7.

Media & references

  • The name of Bruce and the mascot it's based on might be a reference to the movie Finding Nemo, which has a great white shark named Bruce.
