Appearance

Level 421 is a giant children's hospital called "Bruce's Discovery Center" with a shark mascot named Bruce. The level is split into three areas: the M.E.G. base, Bruce's Discovery Center, and the Plastic Mariana.

The M.E.G. base

The M.E.G. base is a small room seemingly owned by M.E.G. containing four chambers of plastic balls with one broken and missing a lot of balls with one color changing in a path throughout the other balls.

Bruce's Discovery Center

Bruce's Discovery Center consists of a bunch of hallways with hospital rooms, posters on the wall, chairs, Mini-Bruces, and multiple Scratchers roaming throughout.

The Plastic Mariana

The Plastic Mariana is a giant ball pit with pillars, playgrounds, four lighthouses, and other objects scattered throughout, with a shark named after the level mascot roaming the ball pit. A room with four basketball hoops can also be reached after turning on the four lighthouses in the area.

Mission

Part 1: The M.E.G. base

Upon entry, simply have a player scan their ID card to open the M.E.G. secured door. Inside the M.E.G. base are four chambers of plastic balls, each one labelled a number 1-4. The fourth chamber is broken with a bunch of balls missing from it, and another color flowing inside. The large door in the room requires a four-color code from a color-coded lock. Pay attention to the colors of the balls in the chambers and the number of each chamber. The color corresponds to the color to press on the lock, and the chamber numbers corresponds to the order from top to bottom they must be pressed. The fourth chamber's color can be determined by seeing what two colors are in there. These colors can be found on a note in the room or down below:

  • Green and red make yellow.
  • Blue and green make cyan.
  • Blue and red make pink.

Whichever color it is, put it in on the bottom row. All of the ball colors are different everytime the level is restarted. If done correctly, the door should open, allowing players to move onto the second area of the level.

Part 2: Bruce's Discovery Center

In Bruce's Discovery Center, a Scratcher can be seen walking by when a player walks near the first room that has a note about surviving a Scratcher. A caged off area with ball pits that noclips one into the Plastic Mariana can also be found and must be reached while avoiding the two Scratchers that patrol the floors nearby it. In order to unlock the gate, there are three flower lights in rooms surrounding the caged off area, with each one aiming at artwork of different looking white colored flowers on the wall.

The flower lights have the colors blue, red, green, and white with each color turning on a different flower's light on the flower light to shine onto the flower artwork, excluding the white light, which resets the lights. The lights can also be combined to make yellow, pink, and cyan. The flowers on the wall must be painted a specific combined color combination, which can be figured out by exploring the level to find hospital rooms that have repeated artwork of the same flowers but with the right colors. The location of these rooms and the flower colors are different everytime. The colors can be made using the light combinations found down below:

  • Red and green make yellow.
  • Blue and green make cyan.
  • Blue and red make pink.
  • Combining all the lights make white.

Each correctly shining flower will cause a light surrounding the gate to light up, with four lights in total with the upper right one always being on. If the flower lights are shining the correct colors, the gate to the caged off area will unlock. Only one of the ball pits can be entered though. Upon entering said ball pit, players will fall down into the Plastic Mariana.

Part 3: The Plastic Mariana

In the Plastic Mariana, there are four lighthouses scattered around the giant ball pit, and the player's objective is to turn all four on while avoiding Bruce, which can be hid from by going inside a lighthouse, on a playground, or a staircase. A lighthouse can be turned on by pressing the switch inside to on, which will cause the light on the lighthouse to enable and start spinning around. If a player walks outside of the intended boundaries of the Plastic Mariana, they will die to sanity effects without the sanity actually being affected, starting off with the first warning effects, and then instantly after, a sanity death will occur.

After turning on four, players must find a Light Snake made up of white balls and follow it, as it will lead to a circle it forms. Walking into the circle will cause the player to noclip into the basketball hoop room. In this room, there are four basketball hoops with lights above them. Players must have plastic balls by the time they get to this room because in order to unlock the M.E.G. secured door, a plastic ball must be thrown into all four hoops. Plastic balls can be obtained by picking them up on the ground in the room, which would be necessary if no plastic balls were obtained previously. Throwing a ball into one will turn its light on. If all four lights are on, the M.E.G. secured door will open, with the entrance to Overgrowth on the other side.

Entrances & Exits

Entrances

  • Go back through the entrance in Overgrowth to get here, although due to checkpoints players can only be in the basketball hoop room.
  • Use an ID card on the M.E.G. secured door in the Bunker and go down the slope in the parking garage area on the other side to get here.

Exits

  • Go back through the entrance to get to the Bunker, although due to checkpoints, if players have reached the Plastic Mariana which enables a checkpoint to the basketball hoop room, they can only be in said room.
  • Get to the basketball hoops room and throw plastic balls into all four hoops to unlock the M.E.G. door and get to Overgrowth.

Trivia

The level

  • This level is one of the only two secret levels to not have an achievement for completing it. The other being the Bunker.
  • This is currently the level with the most amount of entity species in the game, having a total of five.
  • This is the first and currently only secret level to have more card scanners after its entry scanner, and to have a color-coded lock, and its the first and currently only level to have colored notes.
  • This is the second level to have lighthouses in it, the other being Level 7.
  • The three posters found throughout the level say the following:
    • SMILE! WE'RE HERE TO TAKE CARE OF YOU.
    • DIVE INTO THE FUN!
    • STAY CALM! YOU ARE PROTECTED WITH US
  • Scratch marks can be seen throughout the level, which hence the name Scratcher, were probably left by them.
  • The ambiences of this level are reused from Level !~!, Level 0.11, Level You Cheated, and Level 6.
  • This is the first secret level to release after Version 1.0.
  • This level is the first and currently only one to release on its own.
    • This goes against what Fancy said in his 4th Escape the Backrooms devlog, where he stated that he was initially going to release the new levels one by one but decided against it due to not wanting players to play the game for a tiny bit more and then have to wait again and the new levels having been very complete.
  • Despite this level being announced after The Afterparty, the complete Level You Cheated, the Unknown Dark Level, and Level ???, it still released before them.
    • This is the first and currently only time this has ever happened.
  • This level had a dedicated trailer for it.
    • This is the first and currently the only level to have its own trailer.

Lore & theories

  • A description of the level that explains the level's lore was given with the level name reveal, "The M.E.G were researching a new entity - a living, sentient, plastic polymer ball. Now it's escaped into a children's hospital, and a no-clip zone has been located inside Bruce's Discovery Centre. Where will it lead?".
    • In this description, Center is spelled as "Centre", due to Secret Mode being an England studio.
  • A TikTok video from the Escape the Backrooms TikTok account gave some information on the entities in the level.
    • The Scratchers appear damaged due to them being experimented on by scientists, who believed they would die if abandoned, but that is not the case as the Scratchers are still alive.
    • It was said that the Mini-Bruces are neutral but not an instant threat to one's health, which is interesting since they don't seem to do anything.
    • The only information given on Bruce that wasn't survival advice said that it's an entity that looks and acts like a shark and that the archive notes aren't lengthy.
    • Not much research was done on the Light Snake and that it seems to be friendly if players light up its surroundings, which it's entirely non-hostile, and this was hinting at having to turn on all four lighthouses so it can form a circle that a player can noclip through to get to the basketball hoop room.
    • The information was said to come from the unredacted files of the M.E.G. archive, which seems to be a made-up thing by Secret Mode, but it might be or might be related to the computer documents from the computers in The Hub's M.E.G. base.

Bugs

  • Sometimes, scanning an ID card too close to the card scanner at the entrance to the M.E.G. base will cause the scanner to not scan the card.
  • The sound of the Scratcher that walks by at the start of Bruce's Discovery Center is a separate sound from the Scratcher itself, so if a player gets close to it, two whispering sounds can be heard by them.
  • Sometimes, the Scratcher that walks by at the start of Bruce's Discovery Center will not appear.
  • A plastic ball can be found clipped into the ground in Bruce's Discovery Center.
  • Untextured Toys can be found on a stretcher in Bruce's Discovery Center.
  • Stepping into any ball pit will cause the sounds of moving in a ball pit to continue playing for that player when stepping out.
  • If a player is in a ball pit or the game thinks you are, they can look down and be able to grab endless balls from around them.

Media & references

  • The name of the hospital's shark mascot Bruce might be a reference to the movie Finding Nemo, which has a great white shark named Bruce.
  • The "SMILE!" poster is a clear reference to the cover of the movie Jaws.

Level inspiration
