Trivia

The level

  • Out of all the current upcoming levels, this one has the second most amount of information about it, having two pictures revealed of it.
    • The other upcoming levels include The Afterparty as the one with the most amount of information about it, having some information revealed about it and a look at two rooms, the Unknown Dark Level as the one with the third most amount of information about it, only having a blurred picture, and Level ??? as the one with the least amount of information about it, because it's not even known for sure what the level is, if it's this level or one of the other new ones coming and was only mentioned.
  • The bottom text of the posters in the level are hard to read, but the top text says "SMILE!".
  • A caged off area has a sign that reads, "Bruce's ???? ?? Center".
    • Behind this area also appears to be a pool, which would make sense for a place like this.
    • There's a possibility that could be what the rest of the the caption apart of the level teaser that cuts it off at Bruce's, since that's how it starts.
  • Wall art from the liminal room in the Bunker can be seen in the level.

Speculation

  • It is unclear if this is an original level, or a level based on one from one of the Backrooms wikis, but if it is based on a Backrooms level, there's a chance it could be Level 43 - Water World due to the aquatic theming and a suggestion from the Escape the Backrooms Feature Upvote suggesting Level 43, considering that quite a lot of fixes and changes have actually come to the game from the feature upvote although for now, there is no certainty.
